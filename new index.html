About HomeFix Services

Your Trusted Home Maintenance Partner

HomeFix Services is dedicated to making home maintenance simple, reliable and stress-free. We understand that your home is one of your most valuable assets, and keeping it safe, comfortable and well-maintained is important.

We offer a range of home maintenance and repair services, including plumbing, electrical work, AC servicing, painting, carpentry, home renovation, earthing and grounding, and general maintenance.

Whether you need a leaking tap repaired, a fan installed, a room painted or assistance with home renovation, our goal is to make finding the right service straightforward.

We believe in clear communication, reasonable pricing, timely service and treating every home with care and respect. We encourage customers to confirm the work and expected costs before the service begins.

Our mission is to provide dependable home maintenance solutions that help make every home safer, more comfortable and better maintained.
